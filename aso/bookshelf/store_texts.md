## Description (1167/4000)

A shelf of shuffled books. A hidden masterpiece. One quiet puzzle at a time.

Every book spine carries a slice of a famous painting. Drag the books, swap them around and watch the shelves slowly turn into the Mona Lisa, The Starry Night or The Great Wave. When the last book slides into place, the bookcase becomes a gold frame and the artwork goes on display in your own gallery.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag a book onto another one to swap their places. Nothing else on the shelf moves.
• Books that fit together lock into a block. Move the whole block as one.
• Stuck? Hold to preview the original, or use one of three hints per painting.
• Finish in fewer moves to earn three stars.

A MUSEUM IN YOUR POCKET
• Six world-famous works, from an easy 16-book Hokusai to a 50-book Klimt.
• Museum labels with the artist, the year and a surprising story behind every painting.
• A gallery that fills up as you play. Restore them all.

RELAX AND FOCUS
No timer, no lives, no pressure. Cozy wood shelves, brass accents and soft ambient piano. A sorting puzzle for art lovers, jigsaw fans and anyone who likes a calm brain game before bed.

Sort the spines. Reveal the masterpiece.
