# Gem Bounce

App Store subtitle: Aim, Ricochet & Shatter

Google Play short description: Hold to fire. Bounce gems off mirrors, through portals, into pixel pictures!

## Description

One finger. One angle. A shower of gems.

Slide your blaster, hold to fire and watch a sparkling stream of gems ricochet off mirrors, slip through portals and shatter colorful pixel pictures. No aiming skills needed: find the sweet spot and let the bounce do the rest.

TEN TWISTS, ONE RULE
• Mirrors and curved reflectors send your stream in new directions.
• Portals keep your angle: in one side, out the other.
• Hot mirrors overheat, prisms split every gem in three.
• Powder kegs count down to a satisfying KABOOM.
• Hidden obstacles, moving mirrors and key blocks change the route mid-level.
• Stack combos to trigger FEVER mode.

EASY TO START, HARD TO PUT DOWN
Hold anywhere to shoot, release to pause. Every gem that leaves the field is gone, so plan the bounce. Clear the picture and collect three stars.

30 levels across a colorful map, bonus stages and the Booster Lab. Short rounds, no timer, pure satisfaction.

Find the angle. Watch it bounce.
