# Gem Shooter

App Store subtitle: Bounce, Aim & Shine

Google Play short description: Bounce gems through mirrors, portals and hidden obstacles. Find your angle!

## Description

A little move. A brilliant ricochet.

Find the sweet spot for your blaster and send a sparkling stream of gems through a world of mirrors, obstacles and colorful jewel pictures. Watch each bounce, adjust your position and discover a path that works.

EXPLORE DIFFERENT WAYS TO PLAY
• Shatter colorful jewel pictures with a carefully placed stream of ricocheting gems.
• Break numbered blocks one hit at a time and open new paths.
• Send a shot through portals that preserve its angle and speed.
• Reveal the obstacle inside a numbered cloud, then rethink your route.
• Follow moving mirrors and keep your stream on target.
• Hit a key block to unlock a different route.
• Connect several reflections in a single satisfying shot.

MAKE EVERY SHOT COUNT
Move the blaster while firing. Release to pause your stream in the newer challenges. Shots that leave the field are lost. In levels with limited gems, find the route before your bag runs dry.

A colorful level map, sparkling projectiles and bright jewel pictures turn every discovery into a little celebration.

Find your angle. Make it shine.
